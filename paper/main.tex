\begin{abstract}
This paper studies budgeted target-data acquisition for adapting a learned dispatch surrogate to an unseen line-outage topology. Results and conclusions are intentionally left open until the frozen protocol is executed.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}

The provisional contributions are:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item A controlled benchmark for evaluating dispatch-surrogate adaptation under an unseen line-outage topology shift.
  \item A comparison of random, diversity, uncertainty and decision-sensitive target-data acquisition under equal SCED-label budgets.
  \item Operational evaluation using security feasibility, repair magnitude and dispatch-cost regret rather than prediction error alone.
  \item An analysis of whether ex-ante acquisition scores predict the realised downstream value of selected operating conditions.
\end{enumerate}
These statements remain provisional until the literature review confirms novelty.
